Questions, answered.

What is MilesSpace?

A wallet for everyday digital services. You fund it, then use it to buy airtime, data, gift cards, eSIMs and more.

How does the wallet work?

Your balance sits in your MilesSpace wallet. Services you buy are paid from that balance.

How do I fund my wallet?

Sign in, open the Wallet page and choose Add Funds.

Can I buy airtime and data?

Yes. Both are paid for from your wallet.

Can I buy gift cards?

Yes, from inside your account.

What is an eSIM?

A SIM that is installed digitally instead of as a plastic card. Your phone must support eSIM, so check your phone’s settings or the manufacturer before buying.

What are virtual numbers?

Phone numbers you can use without a physical SIM, for supported services.

What if a transaction fails?

Check its status in your transaction list, then ask for help with the details. The refund policy explains how failed purchases are handled.

How do I keep my account secure?

Use a strong, unique password, never share it, and log out on shared devices.

How can I get help?

Use the Miles assistant at the bottom right of this page. Signed-in users can also use the support tools inside the app.
