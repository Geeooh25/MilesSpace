Reconciliation

Compares our internal deposits against the provider's records. This page never changes a wallet. Resolutions run through the ledger with a reason, an audit trail, and (for credits) step-up MFA.
Read-only. Reconciliation surfaces mismatches. It never auto-corrects them. A "recheck" verifies the provider but does not credit the wallet — settlement happens through the deposit webhook flow, which is idempotent.
